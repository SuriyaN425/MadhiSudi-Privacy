Madhi Sudi - Privacy Policy and Support

Effective Date: August 15, 2026

Privacy Policy

The Madhi Sudi app is a devotional tool built to provide access to Tamil hymns. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to your privacy.

Information Collection

The Madhi Sudi app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Our app uses Firebase Firestore as a backend service strictly to retrieve content (such as hymn lyrics and text). This service is used solely for the delivery of app content to the user's device. The application does not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, browsing history, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).
 
Crash and Diagnostic Data
 
The Madhi Sudi app uses Firebase Crashlytics to help us identify and fix technical problems. When the app encounters an error or crashes, Crashlytics automatically collects diagnostic information such as crash logs, device model, operating system version, and app version. This data is associated with a per-installation identifier generated by Crashlytics, not with you personally — we do not collect your name, email address, or any other information that identifies you as an individual, and this data is never used for advertising, tracking, or any purpose other than improving the stability of the app.
 
Cookies and Tracking
 
The Madhi Sudi app does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party advertising or marketing SDKs. Firebase Crashlytics, described above, is used solely for crash and diagnostic reporting — it does not track your activity across other apps or websites.
Children’s Privacy

The application does not knowingly solicit or collect data from children, nor does it market to them.
